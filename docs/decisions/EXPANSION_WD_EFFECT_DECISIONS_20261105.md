# WD effect decisions (owner, 2026-11-05)

Owner answers to the EX-03 H5 proposal. They extend E01–E16 / M01–M08; they do not override them.

| ID | Card | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| W01 | Majestelk | Sacrifice a Hero, then until the owner's next turn **each** of the owner's rolls asks once for `+5×n` or `-5×n`, where `n` = number of active Majestelk activations (two activations → ±10, one popup). |
| W02 | Feral Dragon | Its owner DRAWs 1 each time a **Hero** is sacrificed by any player's effect. A Decoy Doll sacrifice does **not** count (Hero stays); a Maegisty return is not a sacrifice. |
| W03 | Silent Shield | On a **successful** Hero roll it arms for the active player until the end of that turn. Each time a Hero of theirs is SACRIFICED (even when another player forces it) or they DESTROY a Hero, they may search the discard pile for a Hero and add it to hand. |
| W04 | Big Buckley | ATTACK a Monster now, no AP; requirement still applies; counts as an ATTACK for ATTACK bonuses. |
| W05 | Buck Omens | Only the acting player sees the target's hand. The chosen Hero enters the Party directly (no PLAY, no Challenge, no free roll). |
| W06 | Tenacious Timber | Count the Monsters in your Party once when it resolves and choose all Heroes to steal in one selection. |
| W07 | Beast Call | Move face-up Monsters to the bottom (left→right order), flip the top 3, gain +1 AP. |
| W08 | Muscipula Rex | One free **draw-only** credit per own turn, spent automatically by the DRAW action before AP. No new command. |
| W09 | The Noble Shaman | Leader passive (not a Modifier card), once per turn on every player's turn, -1 to a chosen roll. Stagguard blocks Modifier cards only, never this. |
| W10 | Cursed Glove | When another Hero in the party is stolen, the equipped Hero (with its attachments) moves to the thief after it. Mandatory. If the thief's Party is full that Hero and its items go to the discard pile. A move is not a steal. If the steal could not happen (Calming Voice) nothing triggers. |
| W11 | Soul Tether | When any other Hero in the party is really sacrificed or destroyed, SACRIFICE the equipped Hero (a real sacrifice: Feral Dragon/Silent Shield count it). Maegisty/Decoy replacements apply to it. Steal, return and move never trigger it. A Hero is lost only once, so tethers cannot loop. |
